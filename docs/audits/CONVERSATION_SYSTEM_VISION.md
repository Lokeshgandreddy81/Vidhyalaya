# CONVERSATION SYSTEM VISION

## From "Chat Window" to "Conversation Layer"
The traditional paradigm confines intelligence to a rigid box on the right side of the screen. In Cortex, the conversation is a **Layer**, not a window. 

The Conversation Layer is:
1. **Omnipresent:** It lives alongside the terminal, hovers over the code, and integrates into the curriculum text.
2. **Ambient:** It watches the student's progress passively and only intervenes proactively when it detects high hesitation or repeated errors.
3. **Contextually Embedded:** When the AI speaks about code, it physically points to the code. When it speaks about the terminal, the terminal responds.

## The Mental Model
The student should not feel they are "opening a chat". They should feel they are "turning to their mentor". 
The system does not "answer prompts". The system "guides the session".

## Environmental Awareness
The Conversation Layer understands the environment intrinsically:
- **Current Lesson:** It knows the learning objectives.
- **Current Mission:** It knows the exact challenge the student is facing.
- **Current Code:** It reads the live buffer of the editor.
- **Current Errors:** It parses the terminal output in real-time.
- **Current Progress:** It knows if this is the first attempt or the fiftieth.
- **Current Skill Level:** It adjusts its vocabulary based on the student's neural graph.
- **Learning History:** It remembers past struggles.
- **Student Intent:** It knows *why* the student is asking, not just *what* they are asking.
